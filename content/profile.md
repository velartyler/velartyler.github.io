I am a PhD Candidate in the Department of Linguistics at the Ohio State University where I am advised by [Cynthia Clopper](https://linguistics.osu.edu/people/clopper.1) and [Kathryn Campbell-Kibler](https://linguistics.osu.edu/people/campbellkibler.1).  I completed my MA in Linguistic Theory and Typology at the University of Kentucky in 2021, where I worked with [Kevin McGowan](https://vowel.space), [Jennifer Cramer](https://linguistics.as.uky.edu/users/jsfrie0), and [Josef Fruehwald](https://jofrhwld.github.io). 

I am a phonetician and experimental sociolinguist interested in the integration of linguistic and social information in perception. I am also interested in the role of both listeners' awareness and cognitive control in speech perception. My experimental work connects cognitive science, psycholinguistics, and phonetics to variationist and third-wave sociolinguistic theory. 

 I like gardening, exercising, video games, and baking [bread](/posts/bread/).
